Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Fatih Ceylan ("we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use Pictalume, our iOS daily art puzzle app (the "App").

Information we handle

  • Account information: The App creates a guest account for app services. If you choose to sign in with Apple or Google, we receive account details made available by that provider, such as your provider account identifier, email address, display name, and profile image. Apple may provide a private relay email address instead of your personal email address.
  • Puzzle activity: We store completed artwork identifiers and completion times with your account so the App can show your collection and progress across devices.
  • Feedback: If you send feedback in the App, we receive the message, your account identifier, app version, platform, and device language or locale. Please do not include sensitive personal information in feedback.
  • Information stored on your device: App preferences, daily scratch state, and artwork unlocks earned by watching ads are stored locally. These per-artwork ad unlocks are not synced to your account.
  • Advertising and consent: Google Mobile Ads and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) may process device and advertising identifiers where available and permitted, IP address, general device and app information, ad requests and interactions, and your privacy choices. These details are used to provide eligible ads, measure ad performance, prevent fraud, and respect your consent choices. The App does not request precise location for advertising.
  • Analytics and crash reports: Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics may receive app usage and diagnostic information, such as app events, app and operating system versions, device model, installation identifiers, and crash information. Crash reports can include technical stack traces and details about the app state when a crash occurred.
  • Purchases: When you buy or restore lifetime access, Apple processes the in-app purchase. RevenueCat processes transaction and entitlement information and an app user identifier so the App can recognize access. We do not receive your full payment card details.
  • Network and artwork requests: The App connects to our Supabase services and to image hosts that deliver artwork. Those services may receive technical connection information, such as your IP address, to respond to the request and protect their services.

How we use information

We use the information described above to:

  • provide guest and signed-in accounts, puzzle collections, and progress;
  • respond to feedback and support requests;
  • process purchases, restore access, and show rewarded ads;
  • understand app reliability and improve the App;
  • protect the App, users, and service providers from fraud or misuse; and
  • meet legal obligations and enforce our terms.

Service providers and sharing

We share information with service providers only as needed to operate the App and its features:

  • Supabase provides account authentication, database, and backend services.
  • Google provides Google sign-in, Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, AdMob advertising, and UMP consent services. AdMob may share information with the ad technology partners selected in our AdMob settings, as allowed by your choices and applicable rules.
  • RevenueCat supports purchase and entitlement processing. Apple processes App Store transactions and sign-in with Apple.
  • Artwork image hosts deliver images requested by the App.
  • This policy page is hosted by GitHub Pages. When you open the page, GitHub may process technical request information under its Privacy Statement.

We do not sell your personal information for money. Service providers may process information in countries other than your own under their terms and privacy practices.

Advertising choices

Where required, the App uses Google's UMP consent message before requesting ads. Ad availability and whether an ad can be personalized depend on your privacy choices, your region, and Google's ad serving rules. If a privacy choices option is available in the App, you can revisit your ad privacy settings from the More screen.

Data retention and deletion

We keep account information, puzzle completions, and feedback while they are needed to provide the App. Signed-in users can delete their account from More > Account > Delete Account. Deleting the account removes its profile and associated puzzle completion and feedback records from our active database. Guest users can request deletion by contacting us below. Some limited records may remain temporarily in provider backups or logs, or where retention is required by law.

Deleting an account does not remove app preferences or ad-earned artwork unlocks stored locally on that device. Apple and RevenueCat may retain purchase records as needed to provide purchase history, restore access, prevent fraud, and meet legal obligations. Their retention is governed by their own policies.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or a copy of personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. You can use the account deletion option described above or contact us to make a request. We may need to verify your identity before responding. You can also manage ad consent choices in the App where that option is provided.

Children's privacy

Pictalume is a general-audience app and is not specifically directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us and we will take appropriate steps to review and delete it. Where local law sets a different age for children's privacy rights, those requirements apply.

Security

We and our service providers use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect information. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the App or our practices change. We will post the updated version on this page and revise the date above.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Fatih Ceylan at fatihcyln@icloud.com.
