Common questions

You can also find Help & FAQ in the app’s More tab.

How do I restore Unlock Full Collection?

Open More → Unlock Full Collection and tap Restore Purchase. Use the same Apple Account that made the purchase. If it still does not appear, contact support.

What is free, and how do I open another artwork?

A selection of artworks is free to play. For other artworks, you can watch an optional rewarded ad to open one artwork on this device, or make a one-time purchase to unlock the full collection. The purchase is not a subscription.

How can I keep my collection across devices?

Open More → Account and sign in with Apple or Google. Your completed artworks are then associated with your account. Artwork access earned by watching an ad is stored on the original device and does not sync.

How do I delete my account?

For a signed-in account, open More → Account → Delete Account. If you use Pictalume as a guest and need help deleting your account data, email support.
